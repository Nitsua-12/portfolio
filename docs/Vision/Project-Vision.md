# Project Vision

## What this is

A personal portfolio site for Austin Wakefield — currently a Shift Lead at Jersey Mike's (~3 years), studying web development, with a strong Python foundation. The site exists to get him hired for an entry-level web development role, with freelance client work as a secondary path.

## The honest positioning

The original brief this project started from was written for an experienced engineer trying to prove seniority to CTOs. That's not Austin's situation, and the site should not pretend otherwise — an overclaim gets caught in the first technical conversation. The real, more interesting story: an early-career developer who already thinks like a force-multiplier because of *how* he directs AI tools, backed by real leadership experience (shift lead) that most junior candidates don't have yet.

**The site proves this by being genuine work, not by claiming it.** Both flagship projects are built during this engagement, for real, with the AI-orchestration process documented as it happens — not portfolio-ified after the fact.

## Audience

Leads with traditional employers (job/internship hiring managers for entry-level web dev roles). Freelance/direct clients are a secondary audience — the same honesty and craft serve both, but content and tone are written for the hiring manager first.

## Aesthetic direction

Dark, synthwave/retro-neon — arrived at through a specific, unprompted image Austin gave unprompted during Discovery: "retro neon gas station art set at sundown... vibrant pink and blue glows, moody twilight skies, vintage car silhouettes." The [[../AI-Workflow/Decision-Log|Decision Log]] covers how this got reconciled against his other stated preferences (wanting the site to feel "like someone spent good money on it," and "playful/artistic because that's who I am").

The full synthwave palette was chosen over blending in forest green (his personally-meaningful color) for the sake of a cohesive, committed execution of one vivid reference rather than diluting it.

## What must not get lost

- **Faith.** Austin explicitly chose to have his Christian faith stated directly on the site, not just implied through values. When asked what he wants people to remember about him, his answers were "my love for God" and "that man has good character and it comes from God." This is not incidental content — don't soften it in later passes without checking with him.
- **Work-life boundaries.** He explicitly wants to avoid employers/teams that "work him to death." The site's tone toward culture/teams should reflect someone who values sustainable pace, not hustle-culture.
- **Honesty over polish.** Every project shown must be something Austin actually built and can defend in a technical conversation. See the [[../AI-Workflow/Decision-Log|Decision Log]] entry on the herbert-west-landscaping mix-up for why this matters concretely, not just in principle.
